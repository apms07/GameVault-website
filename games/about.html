How reserving works

  1. Open a game page and press Reserve. Your email app opens with the title filled in.
  2. Send the email. We confirm the copy is set aside.
  3. Collect and pay at the counter within two days.

Contact us

Ask about a title or our opening hours. The form opens your email app with the message ready to send.

About this project

GameVault is a portfolio project built with HTML and CSS. The store, products, hours and contact details are fictional. It is the storefront for GameVault, a desktop inventory app written in C# with Windows Forms and MySQL.
